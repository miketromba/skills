# AI Image Prompt Examples for SaaS Websites

Ready-to-use prompts organized by website section and visual style. Replace bracketed `[variables]` with your specifics. Append your brand's style/color/lighting blocks from SKILL.md to each prompt.

## Prompt Modifiers Reference

### Quality & Technical

- `8k, ultra-detailed, sharp focus`
- `professional DSLR quality`
- `photorealistic render`
- `shot on [lens]mm lens, f/[aperture]`

### Mood & Atmosphere

- `premium, sophisticated, polished`
- `warm, approachable, inviting`
- `bold, energetic, dynamic`
- `calm, serene, trustworthy`
- `innovative, cutting-edge, futuristic`

### Composition Helpers

- `negative space on [left/right] for text overlay`
- `rule-of-thirds positioning`
- `centered with balanced symmetry`
- `wide establishing shot, 16:9`
- `tight close-up detail, 1:1`

### Common Negative Prompts

```
No: blurry, out of focus, deformed, mutated, extra limbs,
poorly drawn, text, watermark, signature, cartoon (unless
intended), low resolution, flat lighting, overexposed,
oversaturated, cluttered background, stock photo feel,
generic, cliché
```
